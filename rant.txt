Welcome one and all to the first version of the Pixel Launcher

Features and what even is this --->
1. A working launcher for my broken Samsung J7 Prime to make it loook cool
2. A really cool Vinyl music player as the widget in the Pixel Launcher homescreen which has the following features -->
    1. Right swipe for prev track
    2. Left rotate for next track
    3. A button to open my yeah my audio app SPIGEN Audio T_T
    4. Tap on the vinyl to play/pause music
    5. Support limited to Youtube Music(Yeah I know I should prob fix that in next version)

3. A swipe up to get into the home drawer with a search box that has search menu for apps and also a simple calculator
4. A dock in the bottom to open my quick used apps.
5. A half baked clock and internet data usage in the top left

IDK Just making something to improve my experience on the J7 Prime and live this life IG.
Wanna make something that provides value for the user T_T

Made in Kotlin exclusively on the Android Studio
